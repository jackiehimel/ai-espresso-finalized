## DeepMind just mapped what happens when you change any letter in human DNA

AlphaGenome Atlas predicts the molecular effects of all 9 billion possible single-letter DNA changes across the human genome. It's a reference map researchers can use to understand which genetic variants might cause disease and which are harmless—without running wet-lab experiments for each one.

*Turns genetic diagnosis from experimental guesswork into a lookup table.*
